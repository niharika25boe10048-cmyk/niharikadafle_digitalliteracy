Stay Safe Online – 
Detailed Checklist for College Students


Use strong and unique passwords: Create passwords with a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Avoid using the same password across multiple accounts.
Enable two-factor authentication (2FA): Add an extra layer of security to important accounts like email, banking, and social media.
Never share sensitive information: Do not disclose OTPs, passwords, debit/credit card details, or UPI PINs to anyone, even if they claim to be from a bank or official service.
Be cautious of phishing attempts: Avoid clicking on unknown links, emails, or messages that ask for personal or financial information. Always verify the sender before responding.
Keep your devices secure: Regularly update your phone and laptop software, and install trusted antivirus applications to protect against malware.
Avoid using public Wi-Fi for transactions: Public networks are often unsecured; avoid accessing banking apps or making payments while connected to them.
Check website authenticity: Before entering any details, ensure the website URL starts with “https://” and belongs to a trusted source.
Think before posting online: Do not share personal details like your address, phone number, or travel plans on social media. Maintain a professional digital presence.
UPI Safety Tip 1: Remember that you do not need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money—only enter it when making a payment.
UPI Safety Tip 2: Carefully verify QR codes and payment requests before approving them, as scammers often use fake requests to trick users.
Regularly monitor accounts: Keep track of your bank statements and app notifications to identify suspicious activity early.
Log out from shared devices: Always sign out from accounts when using public or shared computers and avoid saving passwords on them.

Reporting Cyber Fraud in India
Report incidents immediately on the National Cyber Crime Portal: https://www.cybercrime.gov.in
Call the Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930 to report fraud and request quick action to block transactions
